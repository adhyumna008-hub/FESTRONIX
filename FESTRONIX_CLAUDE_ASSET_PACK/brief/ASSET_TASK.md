# FESTRONIX Asset Task Brief

## Purpose

Use this pack to prepare original visual assets for the reusable FESTRONIX annual technology-festival platform. Pulse, Panel and Flux are presentation systems for one platform and one shared content model.

## Read first

Review every document in `docs/` and the reference captures in `references/pulse/`, `references/panel/` and `references/flux/`. Use the captures to understand composition, hierarchy, texture and motion. Do not trace, reproduce, or closely imitate a reference site's artwork, distinctive layouts, logos, or branded elements.

`PROTOTYPE_DIRECTION_RESET.md` is a placeholder because the source file was not available when this pack was assembled. Do not treat it as design direction.

## Asset scope

Prepare an asset proposal for the visual prototype, organized by theme:

- **Pulse:** energetic, premium festival graphics with clean shapes and restrained playful accents.
- **Panel:** sophisticated editorial/comic framing, selective halftone and motion-line textures, and strong legibility.
- **Flux:** atmospheric technological depth, controlled light, and visual elements that can support the three open hero experiments: FESTRONIX CORE, LIQUID FLUX, and TECH NETWORK / PORTAL.
- **Shared:** year-independent FESTRONIX identity support, event category marks, and reusable decorative elements where justified.

For each proposed asset, state its purpose, theme, intended placement, format, dimensions/aspect ratio, transparency needs, responsive variants, and whether it should be static or animated. Keep critical event information and the `FESTRONIX 2K27` wordmark readable. Edition text remains configurable; do not bake the year into the permanent logo.

## Delivery boundary

The master workspace has no approved `ASSET_MANIFEST.md` yet. Therefore, this task is for reviewing the visual references and preparing a concise asset inventory/manifest proposal only. Do not generate or download final assets until the FESTRONIX owner reviews and approves the manifest. Temporary concepts must be clearly labeled as proposals and must not be presented as approved production artwork.

Favor lightweight, accessible, responsive assets. Avoid fabricated sponsors, event facts, awards, or college marks. Do not include third-party logos unless explicitly supplied and authorized.
